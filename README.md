# Hi, I'm David Givondo

I'm an agricultural and biosystems engineer in Nairobi. I build the energy systems and software that put solar power and better data in the hands of Kenyan farmers.

I'm **Lead Engineer at [Precifarm](https://precifarm.com)**, where we pair distributed solar with Neura, an intelligence layer for farm energy and irrigation decisions. I'm also a **Field Engineer at SunCulture Kenya**, designing, installing and commissioning solar irrigation systems on working farms.

## What I work on

- Solar PV and 48 V battery storage: system sizing, mounting and battery racking
- Solar irrigation, from pump selection to commissioning
- Parametric CAD in CadQuery and FreeCAD, with fabrication drawings generated from code
- Data and AI tooling for engineering and farm decisions

## Background

B.Eng in Agricultural and Biosystems Engineering, University of Eldoret. Registered Graduate Engineer with the Engineers Board of Kenya.
